About

I am a Ph.D. student in astronomy at Peking University. My research uses X-ray and multi-wavelength observations to understand how black holes accrete matter, how gas and dust obscure active galactic nuclei, and how these systems vary over time.

I received my B.S. in Astronomy from Nanjing University in 2025. In 2024, I was a visiting research student at Pennsylvania State University, advised by Prof. W. Niel Brandt and Prof. Bin Luo.
