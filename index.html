01. About Me

SW

I am a first-year doctoral student in astronomy at Peking University, advised by Prof. Xuebing Wu. I received my B.S. in Astronomy from Nanjing University in 2025, where I worked with Prof. Feige Wang and Prof. Bin Luo.

My research primarily utilizes multi-wavelength data (especially X-ray) to study the properties of active galactic nuclei (AGN) and explore the physics of black hole accretion. I specialize in X-ray data reduction and the construction of X-ray catalogs. Currently, I am collaborating with the Einstein Probe (EP) FXT team to develop the EP all-sky X-ray source catalog. I also have extensive experience processing data from observatories such as Chandra, XMM-Newton, NuSTAR, and Swift.

02. Research Interests

🔭

X-ray Astronomy

Multi-wavelength spectral and variability studies of AGN using Chandra, XMM-Newton, and NuSTAR observations.

⭐

Weak-line Quasars

Investigating the physical nature of quasars with unusually weak UV emission lines through X-ray diagnostics.

🌀

Obscured AGN & Dwarf Galaxies

Discovering and characterizing heavily obscured low-mass AGN, exploring AGN-galaxy co-evolution at the low-mass end.
